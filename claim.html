Expense Claim

Fill in, print, sign — then log it in Infotech

Enter your trip/expense details below and the official DCI Consultants claim form fills in automatically as you type, ready to print and take to your supervisor for sign-off. Once it's approved, apply the same claim in the Infotech Claims Portal and upload your receipts.

Step 1

Your details

Your in-progress entries are autosaved in this browser only, so a refresh won't lose your work. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Step 2

Trips & expenses

Add one row per trip or expense. If you enter a mileage, we'll suggest an amount at DCI's official rate of S$0.25/km — you can still edit it. Mileage and taxi claims are capped at S$250 per employee per month combined — we'll warn you if you go over. Keep your original receipts for anything that isn't a mileage claim.

Step 3

Print & sign

The form below updates live as you fill in the steps above.
